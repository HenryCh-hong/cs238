A7(v): gross VOI >= 0 ?
====================================================================================================
  min gross VOI over 20000 random (m, Delta, lam, tau, qprev) draws = -4.441e-16 (numerical noise level ~1e-17 relative to G)
  Proof: G(b) = max_q E_b[u(q,r)] and E_b[ E_{b'}[u(q,r)] ] = E_b[u(q,r)] (martingale mean; E[m'^2 + v'] = m^2 + v). Max of expectations <= expectation of max (Jensen), so E G(b') >= G(b).

A7(vi): trivial regimes (s = 1, qprev = 0)
====================================================================================================
  Trade is strictly optimal for belief (m, v) iff lam m^2 - m + (lam v + tau) < 0. The discriminant
  disc(v) = 1 - 4 lam (lam v + tau) decreases in v, and posterior variances v = 1 - Delta are <= 1.
  Hence if 4 lam tau >= 1 there is no trade for ANY posterior variance in [0,1] and every VOI is 0.
  Caution: G(b) = 0 at the prior (disc(1) <= 0) does NOT make VOI zero, because observing a source
  lowers the variance and can open a trade region (demonstrated below).
  lam=0.5, tau=0.0: 4*lam*tau=0.000; max_m G(m;prior)=0.000e+00; max over Delta in (0,0.99] and m of gross VOI = 3.161e-01
  lam=1.0, tau=0.25: 4*lam*tau=1.000; max_m G(m;prior)=0.000e+00; max over Delta in (0,0.99] and m of gross VOI = 0.000e+00
  lam=1.0, tau=0.1: 4*lam*tau=0.400; max_m G(m;prior)=0.000e+00; max over Delta in (0,0.99] and m of gross VOI = 4.885e-02
  lam=0.05, tau=0.05: 4*lam*tau=0.010; max_m G(m;prior)=2.450e+00; max over Delta in (0,0.99] and m of gross VOI = 6.984e-01
  lam=0.05, tau=0.5: 4*lam*tau=0.100; max_m G(m;prior)=2.000e+00; max over Delta in (0,0.99] and m of gross VOI = 4.386e-01
  Large lam: trade needs posterior variance v > (1-4 lam tau)/(4 lam^2) ... i.e. v below the trade
  threshold v* = (1 - 4 lam tau)/(4 lam^2); with lam=5, tau=0.001 (s=1), v* = 0.0098:
  only sources with Delta > 1 - v* (near-full revelation) can have nonzero VOI for a belief in that range.
  Large tau (lam=0.05): the smallest |m| with positive trade value (s units) rises with tau:
    tau=0.01: smallest |m| with trade value = 0.0602  (boundary ~ lam + tau = 0.060)
    tau=0.1: smallest |m| with trade value = 0.1512  (boundary ~ lam + tau = 0.150)
    tau=0.5: smallest |m| with trade value = 0.5661  (boundary ~ lam + tau = 0.550)
    tau=1.0: smallest |m| with trade value = 1.1119  (boundary ~ lam + tau = 1.050)
  Tiny Delta far from the boundary: gross VOI is exponentially small in (distance/sd):
    m=0.575, Delta=1e-04: gross = 0.000e+00
    m=0.575, Delta=1e-03: gross = 0.000e+00
    m=0.575, Delta=1e-02: gross = 6.854e-10
  Tiny Delta at the boundary: gross VOI ~ sqrt(Delta) (first-order kink effect):
    Delta 1e-08 -> 1e-07: gross 3.980e-05 -> 1.259e-04, local log-slope = 0.500
    Delta 1e-07 -> 1e-06: gross 1.259e-04 -> 3.980e-04, local log-slope = 0.500
    Delta 1e-06 -> 1e-05: gross 3.980e-04 -> 1.259e-03, local log-slope = 0.500
